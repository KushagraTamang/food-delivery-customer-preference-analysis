# 🍔 Impact of Discounts and Delivery Time on Customer Preference in Food Delivery Applications

## 📌 Project Overview

This Industry Research Project examines how **discounts** and **delivery time** influence customer preference and satisfaction when using online food delivery applications such as **Swiggy** and **Zomato**.

With the rapid growth of India's online food delivery market, companies compete through pricing strategies, promotional offers, and operational efficiency. This study aims to identify the factors that most strongly influence customer decision-making and provide data-driven recommendations for improving customer satisfaction and retention.

The project combines survey research, business analytics, statistical analysis, and data visualization to derive meaningful business insights.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze the impact of discounts and promotional offers on customer preference.
- Evaluate how delivery time affects customer satisfaction.
- Study customer switching behaviour between food delivery platforms.
- Identify the key factors influencing platform selection.
- Provide business recommendations for improving customer retention and service quality.

---

# ❓ Business Problem

Online food delivery companies invest heavily in discounts and faster delivery to acquire and retain customers.

However, an important question remains:

> **Which factor has a greater impact on customer preference—discounts or delivery time?**

This project answers that question using survey-based research and analytical techniques.

# 📊 Key Findings

### Most Preferred Platform

- Zomato emerged as the most preferred food delivery application.

### Most Influential Factor

- Discounts and promotional offers were identified as the strongest factor affecting customer preference.

### Customer Satisfaction

- Delivery time significantly influences customer satisfaction.

### Customer Switching Behaviour

- 62.1% of respondents are willing to switch platforms for better discounts.
- 55.2% of respondents are willing to switch for faster delivery.

### Statistical Findings

Correlation Analysis

| Variables | Correlation |
|-----------|------------|
| Discounts & Customer Satisfaction | +0.68 |
| Delivery Time & Customer Satisfaction | +0.72 |

Weighted Average Scores

| Factor | Score |
|---------|------|
| Discounts | 4.31 |
| Delivery Time | 4.12 |

# 📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, food delivery companies should:

- Offer competitive discounts and promotional campaigns.
- Improve delivery speed and operational efficiency.
- Enhance customer support.
- Reduce unnecessary delivery charges.
- Improve food quality and packaging.
- Introduce personalized loyalty programs.
- Invest in better order tracking systems.
- Adopt sustainable packaging practices.
